Withdrawal Policy

Production policy

Withdrawal requests are subject to the configured minimum and maximum, available balance, daily limits, verified-referral requirements, account status, and fraud/eligibility checks. A request first reserves the requested amount. Approval does not mean payment has been sent. Approved requests are marked paid only after an administrator records a payment reference. Rejected requests are refunded through the wallet ledger. Payment timing depends on review and the selected payout method.
