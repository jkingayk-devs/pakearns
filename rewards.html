Production policy

Only server-configured tasks can generate rewards. A browser display, advertisement click, share action, or referral link visit is not by itself proof of a completed rewardable task. Rewards are generated and recorded by server-side functions. If a task requires verification, the reward is not earned until verification succeeds. Task limits and event availability are controlled by the database.
